OneColor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-14

OneColor! : Find the Odd Color (“the app”, “we”, “our”) is operated by winsiner. We respect your privacy and built this game to collect as little data as possible.

TL;DR

  • We do not ask you to sign up. No accounts.
  • Your high score and settings live on your device only.
  • We show ads via Google AdMob and offer one-time "Remove ads" purchase via the stores.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

Data stored on your device only

Stored in Capacitor Preferences (deleted when you uninstall):

  • Best scores per mode (Classic / Time Attack / Endless)
  • Sound and haptics toggles
  • Language preference
  • "Remove ads" entitlement state

Children's privacy

This game does not target children under 13 and contains no violence, sexual content, gambling, or scary themes. Age rating: 4+ (iOS) / Everyone (Play).
