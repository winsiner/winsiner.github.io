1. Who we are (Data controller)

The data controller for personal information processed under this Policy is winsiner. For any privacy-related inquiries, contact siner308@gmail.com.

2.3 Information stored on your device

The following are stored locally on your device (e.g., in localStorage, Capacitor Preferences, or equivalent platform storage) and are not transmitted to our servers:

  • Settings and preferences (language, sound, vibration, etc.)
  • One-time onboarding flags (e.g., whether a tutorial has been viewed)
  • Local counters used by gameplay features

Uninstalling the app removes this data.

2.4 Information from third parties

We may receive limited information from the platforms that distribute our apps (Apple App Store, Google Play) related to in-app purchase verification and aggregate, non-identifying analytics. We do not receive your platform account credentials, name, email, or payment method.

7. How long we keep your information

  • On-device data — stored on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data.
  • Purchase confirmation — retained only as long as needed to provide the corresponding entitlement on your device.
  • Support communications — retained for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and for a reasonable period thereafter to maintain support history (typically up to 2 years).
  • Data held by third-party SDK providers — governed by each provider's privacy policy.

8. How we protect your information

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access, including transport-layer encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for any data transmitted between our Services and third parties. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw your consent (where processing is based on consent);
  • Receive your personal information in a portable format;
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at siner308@gmail.com. For advertising identifiers and IP-based data processed by Google AdMob, you may also contact Google directly; in addition, you can opt out of personalized advertising via the device-level controls below.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → turn on “Opt out of Ads Personalization”.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, our processing is governed by the GDPR (or the UK GDPR or Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, as applicable). The legal bases on which we rely are described in Section 3. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10.2 California, United States

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (“CCPA”) and the California Privacy Rights Act (“CPRA”) provide you with rights to know what personal information we collect, to delete personal information, to correct inaccurate personal information, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do not sell personal information in exchange for monetary consideration. The use of advertising identifiers for personalized ads may, however, qualify as “sharing” under the CPRA. You can opt out of personalized advertising using the device-level controls described in Section 9, and you can submit any other CCPA/CPRA request by emailing us.
